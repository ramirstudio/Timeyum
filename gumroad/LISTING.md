## Description

Timeyum recreates the look of a film camera whose shutter has drifted out of phase with the claw that pulls the film down. The exposure happens while the film is moving, so every bright area leaves a streak, a sharp image stays on top, and whatever runs off one edge of the frame comes back from the opposite edge, because it lands on the neighbouring frame of the film. It is the fault an ARRI Timing Shift Box produces on purpose.

Timeyum is a native After Effects plug-in, not a preset built from copies and blurs. It computes the streak as a single exposure across the whole frame, in linear light, so highlights keep their weight. In Exposure blend mode the light that goes into the streak is taken out of the sharp image, so the average brightness of the shot stays where it was.

### What it does

- Three streak models: Fade, Exponential, and a Camera model driven by the numbers of a real camera: timing shift, shutter angle, pull-down angle and claw ease. Move the timing shift from 0 to 100 degrees and the sharp image gives way to the streak, as it does on film.
- Edge behaviour: wrap-around, extend, mirror or black. A frame line gap puts the black line between frames in the streak's path.
- Direction and shape: any angle, symmetric streaks, a back streak, a start offset, a highlight threshold with a soft knee.
- Ghost echoes, vertical frame roll with a visible frame line, smear tint and saturation, chromatic spread between red and blue, breakup between streaks.
- Shake: a repeatable jitter on length, angle, timing, ghosts and roll, plus gate weave. The same frame always renders the same, in any order.
- Input spaces for linear, sRGB, gamma 2.4, ARRI LogC3 and Sony S-Log3 footage.
- 8, 16 and 32 bits per channel, alpha support, multi-frame rendering, CPU only.

### Warp

Switch on Warp and the streaks stop being uniform. Their length and position change from place to place, smoothly and continuously in time, as if they were moving through a fluid.

Three things drive it. A flow field evolves on its own. The footage drives it as well: brightness and motion, with an inertia setting that gives the reaction a memory so it rises and falls instead of flickering. And a pull point that you drag in the composition panel, or link to a null: the image bulges or pinches around it and the streaks grow longer. An automatic target does the same toward the brightest part of your shot and follows it by itself. A control view shows the field so you can tune it.

One licence is for one person. You can use Timeyum on your own computers and on commercial work, and the footage you render is yours. You cannot share, resell or redistribute the plug-in. A studio needs one licence per person who uses it. The full text is in the zip.

### Questions

Does it work on a Mac? No, this version is Windows only.

Does it work in Premiere Pro? It is built as an After Effects effect and is not supported in Premiere Pro.

Do I need a powerful GPU? No. It runs on the CPU and uses all the cores, so more cores means faster renders.

Can I use it in client work? Yes.

Is it affiliated with ARRI? No. Timeyum is an independent product that emulates a look. ARRI and After Effects are trademarks of their owners.
