Timeyum 1.0.0 for After Effects (Windows 64 bit)

Installation
0. If Windows marked the download as blocked, right-click the zip file, choose
   Properties, tick Unblock and press OK before you extract it.
1. Close After Effects.
2. Copy Timeyum.aex into
   C:\Program Files\Adobe\Common\Plug-ins\7.0\MediaCore\Timeyum\
   (create the Timeyum folder if it does not exist; administrator permission is required).
3. Start After Effects. The effect is under Effects > Timeyum > Timeyum, and in the
   Effects & Presets panel.

Updating
Close After Effects, replace Timeyum.aex with the new file, start After Effects.

Uninstalling
Close After Effects and delete the Timeyum folder from MediaCore.

Requirements
After Effects 2026, Windows 10 or 11, 64 bit. No GPU is needed. The plug-in does not
need an internet connection or a licence key, and it has no other files or libraries.

If the effect does not appear
- After Effects was open while you copied the file: close it completely and try again.
- The file must be in a folder under MediaCore (or in MediaCore itself), not in
  an older Plug-ins folder.
- The plug-in is for After Effects only, not for Premiere Pro.
- If the picture at the top of the panel is missing, the reason is written to
  timeyum_log.txt in your temporary folder (type %TEMP% in the Explorer address bar).
  The effect works without the picture.
